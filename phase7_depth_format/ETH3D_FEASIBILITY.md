# ETH3D measured-versus-predicted-depth feasibility

Checked locally on 16 November 2026. Read-only inventory and file/hash checks;
no download, inference, training or cloud allocation was performed. This is a
preparation note, not a new accuracy result.

**Decision:** the scene-scale diagnostic can be prepared from existing clouds.
The RGB-predicted-depth diagnostic needs full images and verified correspondence
before it can produce valid inputs. The frozen depth model is already present.

## Full images and correspondence are missing

The [PCCC acquisition script](../phase3/data_tools/acquire_pccc.py) deliberately
fetches sampled clouds, labels and folds, excluding full-resolution image
archives. The [provenance note](../phase3/data/pccc/PROVENANCE.md) and acquisition
manifest explicitly state that dense RGB/depth images were not acquired.
A project scan excluding environments and Git metadata found no matching ETH3D
capture image files; the ETH3D-named PNGs are report illustrations.

The local [upstream README](../external/Point-Cloud-Color-Constancy/README.md)
describes RAW-linear ETH3D images as a separate asset and points to original
datasets for full images/depth. This records an acquisition lead, not verified
current download availability.

The upstream [generation example](../external/Point-Cloud-Color-Constancy/PointCloud_Generation/Pcd_Generation.py)
contains a 6048 by 4032 input grid, fixed intrinsics (`fx=3406.79`, `fy=3404.57`,
`cx=3040.861`, `cy=2014.4`), depth filling, a depth division by 10, OpenCV image
loading with division by 16383/clipping, and cloud/color resizing. Its active
`scale=256` differs from the released 16 by 16 and 64 by 64 grids. It is an
example with hardcoded source paths, not a per-capture calibration record or a
verified recipe for every released cloud. The acquired assets supply no dense
RGB, exact original-to-cloud sampling map or per-frame calibration metadata.
Grid ordering and sparse XYZ rays alone do not recover the missing full image.

## Concrete prerequisites and next action

1. Identify the original image asset for each of the 195 benchmark capture IDs,
   including source scene, resolution, RAW conversion/color order and processing.
   Acquire and hash those images separately; do not upsample 256 sampled colors
   and present the result as an original photograph.
2. Establish the crop/resize/interpolation and image-to-point correspondence.
   Check sampled original colors against released point colors under a declared
   conversion. Verify camera axes, projection convention, units and invalid
   masks using source metadata; do not infer physical calibration solely from
   the example constants or optimize alignment against illumination errors.
3. Freeze RGB preprocessing for the depth model and the mapping of its predicted
   camera-Z/ray distances to the same sample locations. Preserve the PCCC RGB
   values and illuminant labels across conditions. Declare scale handling in
   advance and fit no predicted scale to measured test depth. Document the
   indoor checkpoint's intended domain and any unsuitable captures before
   comparing performance.
4. Recover physical scene/group identities where possible before validation or
   independent testing; filename folds alone do not establish scene independence.
   Prepare a training/validation rule, matched controls and budget as described
   in [NEXT_STEPS.md](NEXT_STEPS.md), then decide whether to run the experiment.

While image acquisition/correspondence remains unresolved, a separate local
scene-scale input preparation is feasible: all required measured clouds,
illumination labels and folds are present. That preparation would test a
representation hypothesis and would not demonstrate an RGB-only pipeline.
No training or additional expenditure is authorized by this note.
