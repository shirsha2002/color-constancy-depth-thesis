DEFENSE QUESTIONS AND SHORT ANSWERS
Read these for understanding, not as invented claims.

1. What is color constancy?
It estimates the color of the scene illumination. Our current endpoint is a global illuminant RGB vector.
বাংলা: আলোর রং অনুমান করা; depth prediction নয়।

2. Why use depth?
Geometry may help separate useful scene structure from ambiguous color evidence. Our earlier ETH3D comparison showed a benefit, but the benefit did not hold clearly on every dataset.
বাংলা: সব dataset-এ depth উপকারী হয়েছে বলা যাবে না।

3. What did you implement?
Separate RGB and geometry branches, an input-derived scalar geometry gate, matched controls and a reproducible training/evaluation pipeline.
বাংলা: PCCC-র original code ও নিজের candidate আলাদা করে বলবে।

4. Has the new algorithm improved accuracy?
Not established. The gate scored 2.574 degrees on clean ETH3D inputs versus 2.588 for mask fusion and 2.318 for PCCC. Both primary paired intervals include zero.
বাংলা: স্পষ্টভাবে বলবে: এখনো improvement প্রমাণ হয়নি।

5. Is gating itself novel?
No. Gating is established. A contribution would need a justified depth-quality mechanism and reproducible gains beyond stronger controls and prior work.
বাংলা: শুধু gate যোগ করাকে নতুন গবেষণা বলবে না।

6. What does the gate observe?
Input validity, valid-depth fraction and local log-depth disagreement. It does not observe test errors, corruption names or hidden clean depth.
বাংলা: Gate-এর value calibrated reliability নয়।

7. Could a depth boundary be mistaken for bad depth?
Yes. Real depth discontinuities can raise the disagreement cues. That is a limitation and a reason for better quality supervision and boundary-aware controls.
বাংলা: এটি কারণ জানার সীমাবদ্ধতা; স্বীকার করবে।

8. How did you prevent leakage?
Each official fold has 104 training, 26 inner-validation and 65 held-out captures in the pilot. Epoch 100 is primary. Test results did not select checkpoints or hyperparameters. Prior benchmark exposure remains development exposure.
বাংলা: এগুলো নতুন untouched scenes নয়।

9. Why only 100 epochs?
This is a reduced-budget feasibility pilot with matched schedules. It is separate from the earlier 10,000-epoch PCCC study. We need longer schedules and repeated seeds before stronger conclusions.
বাংলা: পুরোনো ০.৯৮ ডিগ্রি আর নতুন pilot-এর ফল একই training budget নয়।

10. Are the damage tests real sensor failures?
No. They are fixed synthetic depth interventions on real captured point clouds. Rendered-scene simulation and independent real sensor validation are still planned.
বাংলা: Noise যোগ করা আর আসল simulation scene তৈরি করা এক নয়।

11. How does this fit the monocular-depth thesis title?
The registered title is retained. The current supervisor-directed module studies RGB-D illumination estimation. I will confirm scope alignment and test RGB-predicted geometry only with verified inputs. I do not claim an improvement in monocular depth accuracy.
বাংলা: এই পার্থক্য ঢাকবে না; স্যারের সঙ্গে scope ঠিক করা জরুরি।

12. Did you use VGGT, Pi3 or MapAnything?
Not in these results. They are potential geometry sources for later experiments, requiring original images, correspondence and illumination-label checks.
বাংলা: ব্যবহার না করলে ব্যবহার করেছি বলবে না।

13. Which GPU did you use?
The earlier 48-model study used Modal T4 GPUs. This new 21-model fusion pilot used my local Intel GPU. The audit used the saved checkpoints; no new Modal training was needed.
বাংলা: পুরোনো cloud experiment এবং নতুন local pilot আলাদা।

14. What will you do next?
Add a learned constant-gate control, budget longer schedules and repeated seeds, then test genuinely simulated scenes and newly held-out real scenes.
বাংলা: বর্তমান test দেখে ভালো ফল আনার জন্য tuning করবে না।

15. Did you write everything without assistance?
I used coding assistance to develop the implementation. I am responsible for understanding the method, checking the evidence and explaining the limitations.
বাংলা: নিজের না-বোঝা অংশ নিজের ভাষায় বুঝে নেবে; বানিয়ে বলবে না।
